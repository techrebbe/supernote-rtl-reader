## First bounded milestone

Determine the smallest viable interactive adapter for the current original-PDF
view. Its Android PdfRenderer bitmap path is the existing rendering foundation,
not evidence of link/text/outline support. Verify concrete available capabilities
before selecting a dependency or attempting a broad renderer rewrite.

Prove one original page in portrait with link destinations, selectable embedded
text, outline navigation and zoom/pan sharing one source-to-view transform. Then
extend that same adapter to both RTL landscape halves and rotation, retaining
active original-page identity. Keep the proven reader available until the new
path preserves the required navigation; do not ship a single-page-only replacement.

PDF link annotations, PDF outlines and printed contents pages are different
features. Resolve direct/named destinations against original page indices and
preserve supported destination semantics, outline titles/hierarchy/order and
correct half selection. Existing validated destination machinery/test vectors
may inform this; do not import converted-page identities or change frozen contracts.
Unsupported destinations/actions must be explicit, never silently page0/omitted.
Do not execute embedded PDF JavaScript, Launch/file/custom-intent actions or
automatically follow external URLs; supported external links need user intent.

Selection is reading/copying embedded PDF text, not writing Supernote highlights
or modifying the PDF. Scans without a text layer are not promised OCR. Zoom must
retain readable source rendering and correct link/text hit testing, not merely
upscale stale pixels. Persist reading/return state in existing app preferences,
not the PDF or .mark. Opening, rotating, zooming and following links cannot
silently change the native writing page or compete with the stock writer.
